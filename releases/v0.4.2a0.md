# Alpha 0.4.2a0 — M1.E3 Headline Access Gate

Alpha 0.4.2a0 adds the first bounded access-stage mechanism to the active M1
candidate specification. It distinguishes a registered headline preview impression
from the subsequent decision to open/click fuller content.

This release does **not** add an internal-attention model and does not propagate
access into belief, engagement intent or sharing.

## What changed

- Add the executable M1.E3 stage:
  `PreviewImpression → Hneg → Paccess → Access`.
- Register four M1.E3 quantities:
  `Hneg`, `PreviewImpression`, `Paccess` and `Access`.
- Compare two nested reference models:
  - M1.E3-NULL: `logit(Paccess) = b0`;
  - M1.E3-A: `logit(Paccess) = b0 + beta_hneg × Hneg`.
- Use a binary, precomputed controlled `Hneg ∈ {0,1}` cue. CEM does not run a
  sentiment model at runtime.
- Add two active evidence-qualified links:
  `Hneg → Paccess` and `Paccess → Access`.
- Add validation patterns `VAL.M1.004`, `VAL.M1.N04`, `VAL.M1.N05` and
  `VAL.M1.N06`.
- Extend the empirical-target schema with platform-native A/B-test counts without
  inventing participant numbers.
- Add a bilingual, responsive M1.E3 comparator under **Understanding → Mechanisms**.
- Integrate the access-stage distinction into Theory chapter 11 with validated
  deep links to variables, evidence, validation patterns and executable code.

## Empirical target and limits

The active directional target is Robertson et al. (2023), based on randomized
Upworthy headline experiments. The filtered confirmatory sample in the main
Results text reports:

- 12,448 experiments;
- 53,699 headline variants;
- more than 205 million impressions;
- 2,778,124 clicks.

The source study models click probability with a multilevel binomial-logit model
and reports a positive standardized negative-language coefficient. CEM uses only
the bounded directional target:

`higher Hneg → higher Paccess`.

The source coefficient and approximate CTR magnitude are **not** copied into CEM.
The reference values `b0=-2.0` and `beta_hneg=0.20` are demonstrative and
uncalibrated.

The evidence registry also preserves:

- the Upworthy archive provenance;
- the 2024 randomization-integrity correction;
- the archive maintainers' restricted reliable-test reanalysis context;
- Nickl, Hills & Lorenz-Spreen 2025 / APS 2026 as preliminary counterevidence in
  a different experimental context;
- the publication's 53,699 versus 53,669 headline-count discrepancy.

These boundaries prevent the Upworthy finding from being represented as a
universal negativity law.

## Scientific boundary

Alpha 0.4.2a0 keeps these stages/constructs separate:

`PreviewImpression != Access != Attention != Encoding != Belief != EngageIntent != Share`.

In particular:

- `Access=0` does not erase the prior PreviewImpression;
- Paccess does not mutate M0 belief or Share;
- M1.E3 does not mutate M1.E1 Aissue;
- M1.E3 does not mutate M1.E2 Pengage or EngageIntent;
- no temporal access trajectory is claimed from the between-condition evidence.

The evidence snapshot is:

`EVIDENCE.M1.2026-09-16.r1`.
