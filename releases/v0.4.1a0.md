# Alpha 0.4.1a0 — M1.E2 Presentation Framing × Prior-Attitude Congruence

Alpha 0.4.1a0 extends M1 with a second, separately testable information stage while
retaining M1.E1 and the M0 baseline.

## Scientific extension

M1.E2 holds proposition meaning and factual compatibility fixed while comparing:

- confirmation: `TRUE that p`;
- refutation: `FALSE that not-p`.

It adds task-specific prior-attitude congruence and compares three nested models:

1. presentation-normalized NULL;
2. frame-only;
3. frame × congruence.

New registered quantities:

- `Fpres`: presentation frame;
- `Gatt`: task-specific message–prior-attitude congruence;
- `Pengage`: active-engagement probability;
- `EngageIntent`: reference active-engagement observable, explicitly distinct from M0 Share.

## Empirical anchors

Aruguete et al. (2024) provide the general semantic-equivalence framing target.
Across 9,512 adults in Argentina, Brazil, Chile and Colombia, confirmation frames
increased aggregate active engagement relative to refutation frames.

Alvarado, Aruguete & Calvo (2026) provide the differential moderation target.
Their supplemental interaction model reports:

- Confirmation × Partisan Congruence on engagement: 0.124, SE 0.040, p<0.001;
- confirmation main effect among counter-attitudinal respondents: -0.010, SE 0.025;
- interaction on likes: 0.130;
- interaction on shares: 0.068.

These values are validation context only. Alpha 0.4.1 does not fit its logistic
coefficients to the published estimates.

## Registered discrimination tests

- `VAL.M1.002`: semantic-equivalent confirmation exceeds refutation in the
  frame-only reference model;
- `VAL.M1.003`: confirmation advantage is larger under congruent than
  counter-attitudinal conditions;
- `VAL.M1.N02`: normalizing presentation frame removes the frame difference;
- `VAL.M1.N03`: M1 active-engagement intent remains separate from M0 Share.

## Scientific boundaries

`Gatt` is not stable ideology, party identity, personality or a global
confirmation-bias score. It is a task-specific relation.

Aggregate engagement in the cited experiments is not M0 Share. Cognitive
difficulty and negative affect are not required mediators in this release.
Platform ranking, attention/click gating and network amplification remain deferred.

## Interface

Understanding gains a second M1 between-condition comparator showing:

- semantically equivalent confirmation/refutation forms;
- congruent versus counter-attitudinal conditions;
- NULL, frame-only and frame×congruence predictions;
- exact `Pengage` values and contrasts;
- empirical target values and interpretation boundaries.
