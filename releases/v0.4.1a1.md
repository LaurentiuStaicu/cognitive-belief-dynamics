# Alpha 0.4.1a1 — Theory Reader and Guided Understanding

Alpha 0.4.1a1 completes the explanatory layer planned for the existing M1
candidate specification. It does **not** introduce a new scientific mechanism or
recalibrate the model.

## What changed

- Complete 16-chapter bilingual Theory Reader inside **Understanding**.
- Explicit epistemic-status separation across empirical, executable, conceptual
  and interpretive material.
- Contextual inspector linking theory terms to variables, modules, validation,
  evidence, application views and release-pinned code.
- Scientific/editorial Phase C.1 audit covering all 20 conceptual modules,
  Romanian terminology, bilingual structural parity and background-source roles.
- Ten-step bilingual **Guided Tour** connecting the real Theory, Mechanisms,
  Scenarios, Registry, Visual ODD and Planning surfaces.
- Stable deep links, browser Back/Forward return, local tour progress,
  keyboard-operable controls and mobile reflow with a compact step selector.
- Expanded Playwright regression screenshots and navigation checks for Theory and
  Guided Tour in Romanian and English.

## Scientific status

The active candidate model specification remains **M1**, retaining **M0** as the
explicit baseline. M1.E1 editorial selection and M1.E2 presentation
framing × prior-attitude congruence remain demonstrative, uncalibrated candidate
mechanisms.

This release does not change M0/M1 equations, coefficients, empirical targets,
the evidence snapshot or scientific numerical outputs. Software tests and the
Guided Tour do not establish psychological validity, population prevalence or
individual diagnosis.
