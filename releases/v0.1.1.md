## Scientific status

The canonical paradigm remains:

**CBD is an event-driven cognitive state-transition and agent-level stochastic dynamical model informed by systems thinking; it is not currently a formal System Dynamics model.**

No model equation, retained benchmark value, threshold or seed changes in v0.1.1. The evidence set remains the same, but its metadata snapshot advances to `EVIDENCE.M1.2026-09-21.r2` after source-level content auditing and correction/qualification of metadata.

M1.E4 retains two distinct synthetic results:

- participant-aware confirmation: 18/18 primary P64_X10 cells meet the 0.80 recovery gate; minimum observed recovery 0.92;
- Phase M protocol robustness: `PROTOCOL_ROBUSTNESS_FAIL`; 3/18 cells below 0.80; minimum recovery 0.56.

The failed Phase M cells remain `ITEM_MODERATE__EVSD`, `ITEM_HIGH__EVSD`, and `COMBINED_ADVERSE__EVSD`.

These results do not identify a human recognition architecture, identify Pencode, validate a human sample size, or authorize participant recruitment.

## Future research boundary

Progressive endogenization and feedback-loop closure is a future research program, not an activated v0.1.1 feature. The first candidate loop is Share -> transmission/network -> delayed Exposure -> Familiarity/Belief -> Action/Share. Any reclassification toward formal System Dynamics or a hybrid architecture requires an implemented and validated endogenous structure.

## Explicit limitations

v0.1.1 does not establish:

- population calibration of the full model;
- human-participant validation of M1.E4;
- identification or estimation of Pencode;
- authorization of participant recruitment;
- a general-purpose truth, prediction, diagnostic or decision system;
- a production-ready end-user application.
