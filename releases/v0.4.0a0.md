# Alpha 0.4.0a0 — M1 Editorial Emphasis

Alpha 0.4.0a0 introduces the first executable M1 mechanism while retaining the
M0 baseline and all M0 scientific regression gates.

## Scientific extension

M1.E1 explicitly separates:

`fixed fact-compatible information pool → editorial emphasis policy → observed sample → issue appraisal`

The reference experiment keeps the underlying pool constant across conditions and
changes only which fact-compatible units are selected.

New registered quantities:

- `Eedit`: editorial emphasis policy;
- `Sobs`: observed-sample balance;
- `Aissue`: issue appraisal, explicitly distinct from M0 truth-belief `B`;
- content valence as a content attribute, explicitly distinct from factuality.

## Empirical anchor

The directional validation target is Tohidi, Haider & Watts (2025),
"Rethinking news framing with large language models", a preregistered randomized
experiment (N=2,141; main analyzed sample n=1,910 across seven events).

The study reports:

- negative vs neutral effect on feelings: -18.5 percentage points,
  95% CI [-21.2, -15.8], Cohen's d=-0.73;
- negative vs neutral effect on the opinion index: -17.94 percentage points,
  95% CI [-21.76, -14.11].

These magnitudes are stored as empirical context only. Alpha 0.4 does not fit its
reference appraisal gain to reproduce them.

## Nested null model

`VAL.M1.N01` disables editorial selection. All conditions then receive the same
full fact-compatible pool and the condition difference must disappear.

This prevents M1 from obtaining the target pattern by altering ground truth,
changing the underlying pool, or directly injecting an attitude difference.

## Evidence boundaries

The target experiment varies information selection by tone; therefore its causal
effect combines selection and tone variation. M1.E1 does not claim that its scalar
selection rule, observed-balance state, or bounded linear appraisal update were
measured or uniquely identified by the study.

Negative information is not treated as false information.

## Interface

Understanding now includes a separate M1 between-condition laboratory showing:

- the fixed information pool;
- negative, neutral and positive editorial selections;
- selected units;
- `Eedit`, `Sobs`, and `Aissue`;
- the nested null condition;
- the empirical benchmark and scientific boundary.

M0's temporal Narrative Laboratory remains unchanged.
